# Caregiving

### **Caregivers for Alzheimer's and dementia face special challenges.**

Caring for a person with Alzheimer’s or dementia often involves a team of people. Whether you provide daily caregiving, participate in decision making, or simply care about a person with the disease — we have resources to help.

![](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/9213aec3-6add-4909-a56b-1ac792994d05/alzheimers-dementia-wandering-sliderimage.jpg)

## [Wandering](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering)

Anyone who has memory problems is still at risk for wandering. Learn the warning signs and get safety tips.


[Get Safety Tips](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/wandering)

![](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/01928193-a1f3-4828-8c6e-0b5233e8559d/alzheimers-dementia-caregiver-help-sliderimage.jpg)

## [Call Our 24/7 Helpline](https://www.alz.org/help-support/resources/helpline)

Talk to a dementia expert for confidential support, crisis assistance, local resources and information in over 200 languages.


[Learn More](https://www.alz.org/help-support/resources/helpline)

![](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/1e52a874-ef61-46b3-a28b-0fbac9b8fc58/alzheimers-dementia-community-resource-finder-sliderimage.jpg)

## [Join Our Community](https://www.alz.org/help-support/community)

Whether in person or online, join one of our support groups and hear from others that truly understand.


[Get Support](https://www.alz.org/help-support/community)

## What to Expect

As Alzheimer’s progresses, your role as caregiver changes. Learn what to expect and how to prepare.

![](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/688f06ae-7d00-46a5-9bba-51e8fa8ffcd9/alzheimers-dementia-stages-behaviors-teaserimage.jpg?ext=.jpg)

## [Stages and Behaviors](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors)

Learn about what changes to expect from each stage of dementia and see what resources are available every step of the way.


[Learn More](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors)

![](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/d8a8071d-c354-4f6f-8503-3ea10a1dcc59/alzheimers-dementia-stage-early-teaserimage.jpg?ext=.jpg)

## [Early-Stage Caregiving](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage)

Early-stage Alzheimer's and related dementia symptoms are mild and the main role of a caregiver is support.


[Learn About Caregiving in the Early Stage](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/early-stage)

![](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/1f0ffbc8-cc19-488d-851c-b602198b6a32/alzheimers-dementia-stage-middle-teaserimage.jpg?ext=.jpg)

## [Middle-Stage Caregiving](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/middle-stage)

During the middle stages of Alzheimer's, the person living with dementia will need a greater level of care.


[Learn About Caregiving in the Middle Stage](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/middle-stage)

![](https://www.alz.org/getmedia/a386be56-a357-4b02-8c32-94778efc4c8c/alzheimers-dementia-stage-late-teaserimage.jpg?ext=.jpg)

## [Late-Stage Caregiving](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage)

The late stage of Alzheimer's usually requires intensive care. As caregiver, your role focuses on preserving quality of life and dignity.


[Learn About Caregiving in the Late Stage](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/stages-behaviors/late-stage)

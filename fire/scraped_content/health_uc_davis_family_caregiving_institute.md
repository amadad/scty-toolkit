# The Family Caregiving Institute at the Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing at UC Davis

Supporting caregivers

## Family Caregiving Institute

Dedicated to the well-being of family caregivers through education and research.

![woman with father with walker](https://health.ucdavis.edu/media-resources/family-caregiving/images/Marquee/Woman-helps-father-with-walker-web.jpg)

The Family Caregiving Institute at the Betty Irene Moore School of Nursing was launched with caregivers in mind — a world-class group of clinicians, educators and researchers who strive to support the more than 40 million individuals who provide care to aging family members or friends in the U.S.

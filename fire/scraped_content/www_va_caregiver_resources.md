**Caregiver Support Program (CSP)**

The Department of Veteran Affairs (VA) Caregiver Support Program (CSP) offers clinical services to caregivers of eligible and covered Veterans enrolled in the VA health care system. The program’s mission is to promote the health and well-being of family caregivers who care for our Nation’s Veterans, through education, resources, support, and services. **[Find Your Local Caregiver Support Team](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/support/New_CSC_Page.asp)** located at every **[VA facility](https://gcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.va.gov%2Ffind-locations%2F%3FfacilityType%3Dhealth&data=05%7C01%7C%7C2bae86f5e346449144e908da8b66051f%7Ce95f1b23abaf45ee821db7ab251ab3bf%7C0%7C0%7C637975567545404666%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJWIjoiMC4wLjAwMDAiLCJQIjoiV2luMzIiLCJBTiI6Ik1haWwiLCJXVCI6Mn0%3D%7C3000%7C%7C%7C&sdata=czBCqG42Zl%2Bq6U1SbLEyQ%2FXz7cjV3UST%2FW7nfvYEKOs%3D&reserved=0) _._**

### **CSP carries out its mission through two programs:**

**\- [Program of General Caregiver Support Services (PGCSS)](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/Care_Caregivers.asp "/Care_Caregivers.asp")**

**\- [Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers (PCAFC)](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/support/support_benefits.asp "/support/support_benefits.asp")**

**- [CSP - Two Programs: What’s the Difference?](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/support/PCS_Program_Difference.asp "CAREGIVER/support/PCS_Program_Difference.asp")**

**Program of General Caregiver Support Services (PGCSS)**

**- [Services](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/Care_Caregivers.asp "https://www.caregiver.va.gov/Care_Caregivers.asp"):** Skills training, mobile support, one-on-one coaching, group support and coaching, self-care, peer support mentoring, telephone support, online programs, and referrals to available VA and community resources.

**-** Visit the **[CSP Teams/Caregiver Support Team Locator](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/support/New_CSC_Page.asp "https://www.caregiver.va.gov/support/New_CSC_Page.asp")**.

**- Eligibility:** PGCSS is available to caregivers of Veterans enrolled in VA health care who served in any era.

**- How do I apply**: **[PGCSS Frequently Asked Questions](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/pdfs/FactSheets/VA-Caregivers_PGCSS_FAQ_Flyer.pdf# "https://www.caregiver.va.gov/pdfs/FactSheets/VA-Caregivers_PGCSS_FAQ_Flyer.pdf#")**.

**[Learn more about PGCSS](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/Care_Caregivers.asp "/Care_Caregivers.asp")**

**Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers (PCAFC) -**(_See Full Eligibility Requirements Before Applying_)

**- [Services](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/support/support_benefits.asp "/support/support_benefits.asp"):** Education and training, health insurance (if caregiver is otherwise uninsured), mental health counseling, monthly financial stipend, respite care and other services.  **[PCAFC Overview Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=huZJSinVlao&t=2s "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=huZJSinVlao&t=2s")**

**-** **Eligibility:** For Veteran and family caregiver eligibility requirements, please visit **[eligibility requirements](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/pdfs/FactSheets/CSP_Eligibility_Criteria_Factsheet.pdf# "/pdfs/FactSheets/CSP_Eligibility_Criteria_Factsheet.pdf")**.

**NOTE:** **If not meeting all PCAFC eligibility requirements, to include being at least 70% service connected**, please visit our **[Program of General Caregiver Support Services](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/Care_Caregivers.asp "/Care_Caregivers.asp")**(PGCSS)

•All caregivers of Veterans enrolled in VHA are eligible for PGCSS, as long as the Veteran agrees to receive care from the caregiver.

**If meeting all PCAFC eligibility requirements please proceed**:

**-** **HOW DO I** APPLY:

• **Review our: [PCAFC Application Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qacHnBqDcw4 "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qacHnBqDcw4")**• **Apply online**: **[Online application PCAFC](https://www.va.gov/family-member-benefits/apply-for-caregiver-assistance-form-10-10cg/introduction "https://www.va.gov/family-member-benefits/apply-for-caregiver-assistance-form-10-10cg/introduction")**.

• **By mail:** Download and print **[VA Form 1010CG](https://www.va.gov/find-forms/?q=10-10CG "https://www.va.gov/find-forms/?q=10-10CG").**

• **In person:** Bring your completed **[VA Form 10-10CG](https://www.va.gov/find-forms/?q=10-10CG "https://www.va.gov/find-forms/?q=10-10CG")** to your local CSP Teams/Caregiver Support Coordinators.

**[Learn more about PCAFC](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/support/support_benefits.asp "/support/support_benefits.asp")**

**Veteran Requirements for Caregiver Program**

**-** Enrolling in VA Health Care: A Veteran must be enrolled in VA health care, or is a Service member on active duty undergoing a medical discharge.  Veterans can apply for VA health care benefits by completing and following submission instructions here  **[(VA Form 10-10EZ)](https://www.va.gov/find-forms/about-form-10-10ez/ "https://www.va.gov/find-forms/about-form-10-10ez/")**.

**-**Find out how the Veteran can **[apply for VA health care](https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/ "https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/")** [.](https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/ "https://www.va.gov/health-care/how-to-apply/")

**Questions about Caregiver Support Program**

**- Call:** the **[Caregiver Support Line](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/help_landing.asp "/help_landing.asp") (CSL)**at toll-free,**1-855-260-3274**

**-** Visit the **[CSP Teams/Caregiver Support Team Locator](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/support/New_CSC_Page.asp "/support/New_CSC_Page.asp")** to connect.

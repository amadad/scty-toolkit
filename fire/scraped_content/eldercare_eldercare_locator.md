## Protect Yourself Against Financial Scams

![Monthly Feature](https://eldercare.acl.gov/Public/images/Home%20for%20the%20Holidays%202024%20image.jpg)

While financial scams and fraud happen throughout the year, the holidays are a predictable time to see an increase. More online shopping and phone or email donation appeals mean more opportunities for fraud. It is also an emotional time of year for many people, especially those who may feel lonely or isolated, which scammers can exploit.

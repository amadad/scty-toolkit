Here's the comprehensive, structured list of authoritative resources, categorized and simplified for easy integration and quick reference:

## Government Resources
- **CDC Caregiving Resources**
  - [CDC Caregiving Portal](https://www.cdc.gov/caregiving/)
  - Actionable, evidence-based guidance on caregiver health, stress management, and care planning.

- **National Institute on Aging (NIA)**
  - [NIA Caregiving Toolkit](https://www.nia.nih.gov/toolkit/caregiving)
  - Downloadable worksheets for organizing caregiving responsibilities effectively.

- **MedlinePlus Caregiving**
  - [MedlinePlus Caregivers Overview](https://medlineplus.gov/caregivers.html)
  - Vetted resources on caregiver health, stress, and long-distance caregiving.

- **Administration for Community Living (ACL)**
  - [ACL Caregiver Support Programs](https://acl.gov/programs/support-caregivers)
  - National Family Caregiver Support Program details, RAISE Family Caregiving Advisory Council, and National Strategy resources.

- **VA Caregiver Support Program**
  - [VA Caregiver Resources](https://www.caregiver.va.gov/)
  - Support services and resources tailored specifically for veteran caregivers.

- **Alzheimers.gov**
  - [Alzheimer’s and Dementia Caregiving Resources](https://www.alzheimers.gov)
  - Practical tips for dementia caregivers, including home safety, managing behaviors, and local support.

- **Eldercare Locator (Administration on Aging)**
  - [Eldercare Locator](https://eldercare.acl.gov)
  - Directory connecting caregivers with local, vetted support services.

- **Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)**
  - [Nursing Home Compare](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/nursing-home-compare-ed7b0)
  - [Home Health Provider Data](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/search?theme=Nursing%20homes%20including%20rehab%20services)
  - Comprehensive provider information for care decisions.

## Academic Institutions
- **University of Pittsburgh Caregiving Research Center**
  - [Center for Caregiving Research](https://www.healthpolicyinstitute.pitt.edu/center-for-caregiving)

- **Family Caregiving Institute at UC Davis Health**
  - [UC Davis Family Caregiving Institute](https://health.ucdavis.edu/family-caregiving/)

- **Rosalynn Carter Institute for Caregivers**
  - [Rosalynn Carter Institute](https://rosalynncarter.org)

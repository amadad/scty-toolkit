{"answer":"# Most Sought After Grants from the U.S. Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Program\n\nThe U.S. Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) program offers a variety of grants aimed at fostering innovation and commercialization of new technologies. Here are some of the most sought-after grants within the SBIR program:\n\n## 1. Omnibus Solicitations\n- **Overview**: Omnibus Solicitations are researcher-initiated ideas proposed via the SBIR and STTR Omnibus grant solicitations. These funding opportunities do not specify a topic but link to identified topics of interest for each participating awarding component.\n- **Popularity**: Most small business applications to the National Institutes of Health (NIH) are submitted to the Omnibus solicitations, making them highly sought after [[1]](https://seed.nih.gov/small-business-funding/find-funding/sbir-sttr-funding-opportunities#:~:text=These%20funding%20opportunities%20do%20not%20specify%20a%20topic%2C%20though%20they%20link%20to%20identified%20topics%20of%20interest%20for%20each%20participating%20awarding%20component).\n\n## 2. Specific Funding Opportunities\n- **Overview**: These include Requests for Applications (RFAs) and Program Announcements with Set-aside funds (PASs), which focus on specific research areas and have dedicated funding in the Institute/Center's budget.\n- **Popularity**: These targeted solicitations are popular because they provide focused funding opportunities for specific research areas [[2]](https://seed.nih.gov/small-business-funding/find-funding/sbir-sttr-funding-opportunities#:~:text=Some%20SBIR/STTR%20grant%20solicitations%20are%20focused%20on%20specific%20research%20areas).\n\n## 3. National Science Foundation (NSF) SBIR/STTR Program\n- **Overview**: The NSF SBIR/STTR program offers up to $2 million in seed funding and takes zero equity in the company. It funds startups across various technology areas and markets, including artificial intelligence, energy, medical devices, robotics, and semiconductors.\n- **Popularity**: This program is highly sought after due to its substantial funding and broad support for diverse technology areas. It funds about 400 companies each year and encourages participation from women, people of color, and first-time entrepreneurs [[3]](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/#:~:text=National%20Science%20Foundation).\n\n## 4. Department of Defense (DoD) SBIR Program\n- **Overview**: The DoD SBIR program is a significant source of funding for small businesses developing technologies with potential defense applications.\n- **Popularity**: Given the substantial budget and the critical nature of defense-related innovations, this program is highly competitive and sought after [[4]](https://blog.mide.com/how-sbir-grants-can-help-or-hurt-your-small-business#:~:text=Department%20of%20Defense%20%28DoD%29).\n\n## 5. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) SBIR Program\n- **Overview**: The HHS SBIR program, particularly through the NIH, funds a wide range of health-related research and development projects.\n- **Popularity**: This program is highly sought after due to the broad scope of health-related research areas it covers and the significant funding available [[5]](https://blog.mide.com/how-sbir-grants-can-help-or-hurt-your-small-business#:~:text=Department%20of%20Health%20and%20Human%20Services%20%28HHS%29).\n\n## 6. NCI SBIR Innovative Concept Award\n- **Overview**: The National Cancer Institute (NCI) SBIR Innovative Concept Award offers $300,000 to small businesses developing highly innovative and transformative technologies in pediatric and rare cancers.\n- **Popularity**: This award is highly sought after due to its focus on high-risk, high-reward projects and the potential to create new scientific paradigms [[6]](https://sbir.cancer.gov/small-business-funding/contracts/innovative-concept-award#:~:text=The%20NCI%20Small%20Business%20Innovation%20Research%20%28SBIR%29%20Innovative%20Concept%20Award%20offers%20%24300%2C000%20to%20small%20businesses%20developing%20highly%20innovative%20and%20transformative%20technologies%20that%20have%20the%20potential%20to%20create%20new%20scientific%20paradigms%20and%20establish%20entirely%20new%20and%20improved%20approaches%20to%20significantly%20improve%20research%2C%20prevention%2C%20detection%2C%20and%20care%20in%20pediatric%20and%20rare%20cancers).\n\n## 7. NCI SBIR Phase IIB Bridge Award\n- **Overview**: This award supports the next stage of development for federally funded Phase II projects, moving them toward commercialization.\n- **Popularity**: The Phase IIB Bridge Award is popular because it provides critical funding to bridge the gap between early-stage development and commercialization [[7]](https://sbir.cancer.gov/small-business-funding/grants#:~:text=The%20SBIR%20Bridge%20Award%20supports%20the%20next%20stage%20of%20development%20for%20federally%20funded%20Phase%20II%20projects%2C%20moving%20them%20toward%20commercialization).\n\n## 8. Commercialization Readiness Pilot Program (CRP)\n- **Overview**: The CRP is designed to help small businesses transition from Phase II to commercialization by providing additional funding and support.\n- **Popularity**: This program is sought after because it offers additional resources to help small businesses bring their innovations to market [[8]](https://seed.nih.gov/small-business-funding/find-funding#:~:text=Commercialization%20Readiness%20Pilot%20Program%20%28CRP%29).\n\n## 9. Small Business Transition Grant for New Entrepreneurs\n- **Overview**: This grant provides funding opportunities specifically for new entrepreneurs transitioning into the small business sector.\n- **Popularity**: It is highly sought after by new entrepreneurs looking for support to establish their businesses and develop their innovations [[9]](https://seed.nih.gov/small-business-funding/find-funding#:~:text=Small%20Business%20Transition%20Grant%20for%20New%20Entrepreneurs%20Notices%20of%20Funding%20Opportunities%20%28NOFOs%29).\n\nBy targeting these highly sought-after grants, small businesses can access substantial funding and support to develop and commercialize their innovative technologies.","search_results":[{"url":"https://seed.nih.gov/small-business-funding/find-funding/sbir-sttr-funding-opportunities#:~:text=These%20funding%20opportunities%20do%20not%20specify%20a%20topic%2C%20though%20they%20link%20to%20identified%20topics%20of%20interest%20for%20each%20participating%20awarding%20component","name":"SBIR and STTR Funding Opportunities","snippet":"2021-09-12 | Omnibus Solicitations (Parent Announcements) which are researcher-initiated ideas proposed via the SBIR and STTR Omnibus grant solicitations.
